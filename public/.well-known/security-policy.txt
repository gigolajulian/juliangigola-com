Reporting a security problem on juliangigola.com

I run this site myself. If you find a way to read someone else's booking,
gallery, contract or message, to pay less than a session costs, to send
mail as the site, or to change what the site shows, please tell me.

How to report
Email hello@juliangigola.com with "Security" in the subject. Say what you
found, the steps to see it, and the page or address involved. A screenshot
helps. Please leave out any client's personal details you came across.

What happens next
I reply within three days to say I have it. I fix real problems as fast as
I can, usually within two weeks, and tell you when the fix is live. I credit
you if you want, by the name you give.

Please do not
- read, change or delete data that is not yours beyond what shows the problem
- book, pay, or send forms or mail at volume, or slow the site down
- test on other people's accounts or galleries
- share the problem before it is fixed

If you act in good faith and keep to this, I will not take action against
you for the research.
