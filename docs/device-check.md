# iPad check (10 min)

The Windows browsers are checked by jg-QA. This is the only part that needs your hands.

1. On the iPad, start a screen recording (Control Center).
2. Open https://juliangigola.com/?debug
3. Use the site normally for 10 minutes: homepage swipes, the portfolio, open a project and come back, one discipline page, rotate once.
4. Stop the recording and send it over. Nothing to fill in.

jg-QA watches the recording for the ghost title, the Sessions dark flash, a glide-back, and swipes that miss.
