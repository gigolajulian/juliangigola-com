# Design System: Julian Gigola

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Darkroom Wall"**

**Direction**
- **Palette, 60/40 (Julian, 2026-11-22), averaged per page:** not a floor per screen; today's layouts already meet it on average, so it guides new work and is not a reason to redesign. Photographs take 60% of a work page (home, /portfolio, projects); text on the ground (Warm Charcoal / Cold Paper) takes 40%. Booking, contact and legal pages may flip. All color comes from the photos. Teal stays under 1%, marks state only (focus, the current step) and never fills an area. Check: hide the photos, and the page should read as near-black with type.
- **Type:** Inter Tight 900 shouts, IBM Plex Mono whispers. Geist Mono for the hero tagline only.
- **Layout:** full bleed, held stages on the home, square UI.
- **References:** to pick, 3 to 5 of Julian's own frames.
- **Never:** glow, neon, decorative gradients, shadows under cards, a second accent, the eye mark.
- **Standing checks:** see Do's and Don'ts.

The photographs are the room's only color and its only spectacle. Everything else is the wall they hang on: a near-neutral ground, hairlines instead of boxes, and type in two registers. Titles shout once in Inter Tight Black, set tight; everything else speaks in small IBM Plex Mono. The interface is a quiet frame for reaching an inquiry or a booking.

Dark is the default and light is a second world, each tuned on its own terms and never inverted. The theme follows the visitor's system, and the half-disc toggle overrides it for the session. Type and scrims laid over a photograph stay light in both themes.

V3 is square. UI corners are 0, photographs take 4px, the About portrait 8px, and the only pill is the filter chip. Motion is a few authored moments (the hero crossfade, the logo strip drift, the glass header arriving), all off under reduced motion. Several pre-V3 surfaces still ship (the discipline strips with their rail, the commission booking card at /book/<commission>, the contact plate); they keep their own corners and are described under Components so they are not mistaken for drift.

**Motion tokens** (app/styles/tokens.css on origin/next): three curves only. `out` is `--ease-out-strong` (enter, leave, answer a click), `move` is `--ease-in-out-strong` (something travelling), `drawer` is `--ease-drawer` (page and sheet slides, line 84). No ease-in. Six durations, each with one job (lines 236 to 241): press 100ms, hover 150ms, small 250ms (menus, notices, chips, every exit), page 400ms, photo 600ms (a photo travelling), show 1000ms (splash and hero crossfade only). An exit takes about two thirds of its entrance. `--ease-rubber` (line 115) is only for a strip's release past an end. Two one-offs are allowed outside the tokens: the scroll reveal on phone and tablet (0.7s fade, 0.9s rise on cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1), components/v4/home/motion/reveal.css) and the header frost (0.4s in, 0.3s out, app/styles/navfx.css). Values are in the sidecar's `extensions.motion`.

**The Fail Open Rule.** Content is visible by default. A block hides for its reveal only after the observer has seen it (components/v4/home/motion/reveal.ts), so a failed observer, a crawler or an old WebView still shows everything.

**Mark:** the JG monogram (`logo/kit/icon.svg`, `icon-512.png`; `app/icon.svg`) is the tab icon, the share image and the mark anywhere one is needed. The eye is retired.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Photographs lead; the interface recedes.
- Two faces only: Inter Tight 900 for the name and every title, IBM Plex Mono for everything else. The V4 hero tagline in Geist Mono is the one exception.
- The name is always one line, "Julian Gigola" in title case.
- Small text is uppercase mono at 0.14em; prose is sentence case.
- Square UI. Filled means primary, one filled button per view.
- One chromatic accent (teal), for focus, the current step and the picked option. Never decoration.

## Colors

A near-achromatic ground (chroma 0.003 to 0.005) in two tuned themes, with a single teal accent.

### Primary
- **Gallery Ink** (`ink-dark` / `ink-light`): text, the filled button, the selected chip's border. In each theme it is close to the other theme's ground.

### Secondary
- **Darkroom Teal** (`accent-dark` / `accent-light`): focus outlines, the 2px current-step and current-tab line, the hero's live tick, the focused field's underline. Light was darkened to 0.47 for contrast on paper.

### Tertiary
- **Live Green** (`live-dark` / `live-light`): Booked and Sent, always with a word and a check.
- **Signal Red** (`destructive-dark` / `destructive-light`): field errors, at 55% as the error box border.

### Neutral
- **Warm Charcoal** (`ground-dark`) / **Cold Paper** (`ground-light`): the page.
- **Lifted Plate** (`card-*`, `popover-*`, `secondary-*`, `muted-*`): empty photo slots, the selected view toggle, the booking card. Never outlined to lift.
- **Muted Ink** (`muted-ink-*`): labels, captions, counts.
- **Artist Line** (`artist-line-*`): the muted ink a quarter of the way toward full ink, for the artist line under a back cover's title. It stays second to the title and clears 4.5:1 under the caption's 0.85 dim.
- **Service Dim** (`service-dim-*`): unlit service rows, ink mixed 54% into the ground in dark and 64% in light.
- **Nav Teal** (`nav-teal`): the current page in the phone menu drawer over its frost.
- **Line** (`line-dark`, `hairline-light`): every V3 rule and box edge. The legacy shadcn border (`hairline-dark`) sits a step fainter at 11%.
- **On Photo** (`on-photo`, `mute-on-photo`, `line-on-photo`): text, muted text and rules over a photograph, the same in both themes.
- **Scrim** (`scrim`): the gradient under type on a photograph, about 78% to 0.
- **Night** (`night`): the ground under photos and film, and dark text on a light photo button, in both themes.
- **Rail Ink** (`rail-ink-*`) and the two **Cover Tones** (`cover-tone-*`): legacy portfolio rail names over borrowed color, and the load colors of the Motion and Cover art covers.

### Named Rules
**The One Accent Rule.** Teal marks focus, the current step and the picked option, never a fill, a status or decoration.

**The Tuned Twins Rule.** A color added to one theme is added to the other, re-set for that ground's contrast, not mirrored.

**The Photo Is Always Night Rule.** Anything laid on a photograph uses the on-photo set and the scrim, whatever the theme. Two exceptions ship. The V4 hero name and its tagline take the theme's own ink, no shadow, on a band of the page color along the foot of the hero, in both themes (`.mt-name` in app/styles/mtscroll.css); the bands are the lightest that keep the name at 4.5:1. The header under its frost takes the theme's ink too: the frost is a 62% veil of the page color, so dark ink in light and light ink in dark hold at least 6:1 over any photo, and every word shares it.

### Named Rules
**The Two Registers Rule.** Type either shouts (Inter Tight 900, tight, large) or whispers (small mono). No third family, no weight in between. One exception ships: the V4 hero tagline in Geist Mono 500. It is a mono, so it stays in the whisper register.

**The One Line Name Rule.** "Julian Gigola" sits on one line wherever it appears. Size it down before letting it wrap.

**The Fit the Line Rule.** A name that must hold one line is fitted between a documented floor and ceiling, never wrapped or truncated.

## Layout

Pages are full-bleed columns with a side gutter of `max(16px, safe-area)`. Photos run edge to edge. On a phone (to 700px) sections share one 48px rhythm; on desktop sections open with a 96px head and grids sit 72px under their heading. Sizes in a section scale on `--v3u` (1vw) inside `clamp()` rather than stacks of breakpoints. Breakpoints in use: 700px (phone), 800/801px (tablet stepper), 1100px portrait tablets, 1600px big screens where the Sessions names grow and the photo fits the window at 4:5.

**The 200% Zoom Rule.** Under `max-width: 299.98px` (a phone at 200% zoom) rows wrap, grids drop to one column (the home masonry to two), long lines wrap and nothing truncates or leaves the screen. No link hides by width or pointer alone; only a decorative label may (the footer's San Jose label). Legal shows at every width.

/portfolio shows a tools row between two hairlines (search left, Grid, Strip and Color views right), then the discipline chips (one scrolling row on a phone, with an edge fade and a progress line). Grid is 4/3/2 columns of 4:5 covers. Strip turns the wheel sideways on desktop and iPad and swipes natively on a phone. The stepper (booking and inquiry) is content left, sticky photo right at 44%; on a phone the photo becomes a strip on top with a sticky action bar.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat. Surfaces lift by a few points of lightness, not by shadows. The one floating material is the glass header: the ground at 47% over a 24px backdrop blur at 1.8 saturation, a 0.5px line under it, appearing once the hero name scrolls under it (always on inner pages). Over a photo it frosts to 62% (below). Shadows in V3 are only line work drawn with `box-shadow`: the 2px inset accent under a current tab, the 1px accent under a focused field. The film numbers' halo is the one other shadow.

**Header frost (every page with a photo under the bar).** Over a photo the bar has no ground of its own, and no one ink reads on every photo. The frost comes up (62% veil, 0.4s in) while any header word would fall under 4.5:1 without it, checked at 6:1 for margin and a little below the bar on the way down. Under frost the ink is the theme's ink, fixed and never sampled. Once up it stays past the hero; it leaves only back near the top, after 1.2s with nothing failing, fading out over 0.3s.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Plate Rule.** No drop shadows under content, no inner shine, no glow. One exception ships: the home film numbers carry a tight dark halo (stacked text-shadows from 2px to 10px) with no plate or patch behind them, so they read on the palest frame.

## Shapes

Square is the default for UI: buttons, fields, the error box, tabs, the call dialog (4px), hairline grids. Photographs, photo tiles, the reference panel and the selected view toggle take 4px; the About portrait and the legacy covers (`--radius-photo`) take 8px. The filter chip is the only V3 pill (999px). A scrollbar thumb is 4px on the page and 3px in the notes field. Legacy surfaces keep their corners: the booking card 12px with a 0.5px hairline, the contact form's glass plate 16px with 15px for what sits inside its 1px border, small thumbnails 6px, the picker's boxes 2px. A pad behind a photo keeps its corner concentric: the photo's radius plus the pad's outset (0.75rem, the card step).

**The Pill Means Filter Rule.** In V3 a pill is a filter chip and nothing else. Buttons are square.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** let the photograph carry the color; keep chrome within chroma 0.005 except the teal accent and the two statuses.
- **Do** set every title in Inter Tight 900 at -0.05em on a role from the ramp, and keep the name on one line.
- **Do** use the label style (11px mono, 0.14em, uppercase) for small text, numbers in tabular figures.
- **Do** keep one filled button per view; pair it with outline buttons.
- **Do** use the on-photo set and the scrim for anything on a photograph, in both themes.
- **Do** design each new surface in both themes before calling it done.
- **Do** turn motion off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Do** keep content visible by default; hide a block for its reveal only after the observer has seen it.
- **Do** use the JG monogram from `logo/kit/` wherever a mark is needed.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use a pill for anything but a filter chip in V3.
- **Don't** use glow, neon, gradients as decoration, or shadows under cards.
- **Don't** put a decorative eyebrow or kicker above a title; only the audience lines over the doors and the service-page keyword line stay. They are not decoration (Julian, 2026-11-11): the audience line over each door ("For art directors and artists", "For private clients", "Published and commissioned by" over the client strip), and the keyword line over a service page's h1 (`kicker` in `lib/booking/booking.ts`, it carries search terms).
- **Don't** add a third type family or a weight between the 900 titles and the mono. The V4 hero tagline's Geist Mono 500 is the one exception.
- **Don't** set type below 9px.
- **Don't** use the teal accent as a fill or for status.
- **Don't** make logos react to hover.
- **Don't** outline a surface to lift it; step its lightness instead.
- **Don't** hide a link by width or pointer; only a decorative label may hide.
- **Don't** hide content until a script shows it.
- **Don't** use the eye mark.

### Standing checks
- Text holds 4.5:1 over photos in both themes, header words included (large display text 3:1). The header reports each word's worst contrast in `data-cr`.
- At 200% zoom on a phone (300px wide) nothing is cut off, hidden or scrolls sideways.
- With scroll observers off, every block is visible.
- Both themes and reduced motion pass before a surface is done.
