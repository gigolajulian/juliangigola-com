# Design System: Julian Gigola

<!-- impeccable:design-map -->
Name: Julian Gigola
Colors: primary: ink-dark oklch(0.955 0.004 80); secondary: accent-dark oklch(0.72 0.095 195); tertiary: live-dark oklch(0.74 0.115 152); neutral: ground-dark oklch(0.145 0.003 70) (+28 more in the sidecar)
Fonts: Inter Tight (hero-name, page-title, section-title, project-title, index-row, reach-title, menu-name, tile-name, headline-phone, wordmark, wordmark-notice, display, title, cal-month-phone, confirm-note, fitted-strip, fitted-strip-grid, fitted-cover, fitted-chapter, fitte...
Spacing: gutter-v3 max(16px, env(safe-area-inset-left)), gutter 1.5rem, gutter-wide 2.5rem, rhythm-phone 48px, head-desktop 96px, grid-gap-desktop 72px, page-max 100rem
Radius: none 0, hair 0.125rem, scroll-thumb 3px, photo 4px, thumb 6px, portrait 8px, card 12px, plate-inner 15px, plate 16px, pill 9999px
North Star: "The Darkroom Wall"
Rules:
- The One Accent Rule. Teal marks focus, the current step and the picked option, never a fill, a status or decoration.
- The Tuned Twins Rule. A color added to one theme is added to the other, re-set for that ground's contrast, not mirrored.
- The Photo Is Always Night Rule. Anything laid on a photograph uses the on-photo set and the scrim, whatever the theme.
- The Two Registers Rule. Type either shouts (Inter Tight 900, tight, large) or whispers (small mono).
- The One Line Name Rule. "Julian Gigola" sits on one line wherever it appears.
- The Fit the Line Rule. A name that must hold one line is fitted between a documented floor and ceiling, never wrapped or truncated.
- The Flat Plate Rule. No drop shadows under content, no inner shine, no glow.
- The Pill Means Filter Rule. In V3 a pill is a filter chip and nothing else.
Don't:
- Don't use a pill for anything but a filter chip in V3.
- Don't use glow, neon, gradients as decoration, or shadows under cards.
- Don't put a decorative eyebrow or kicker above a title; only the audience lines over the doors and the service-page keyword line stay.
- Don't add a third type family or a weight between the 900 titles and the mono.
- Don't set type below 9px.
- Don't use the teal accent as a fill or for status.
- Don't make logos react to hover.
- Don't outline a surface to lift it; step its lightness instead.
